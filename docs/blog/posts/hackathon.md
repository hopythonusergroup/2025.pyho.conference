# 🚀 Announcing the PyHo 2025 Pre-Conference Hackathon – Build. Break. Innovate.
Get ready, makers and innovators — the **PyHo 2025 Pre-Conference Hackathon** is here!  
Join us on **October 22, 2025**, right before the main conference days (October 24–25), for two thrilling days of coding, collaboration, and creativity in Ho, Ghana.  
<!-- more -->

This year’s pre-conference lineup brings **two major experiences** under one roof:

## 🧩 Django Day – Learn. Build. Connect.
A dedicated Django session day — exploring advanced workflows.  
Whether you’re new to Django or looking to level up your skills, **Django Day** is your chance to learn, experiment, and connect with developers from across the region.

Expect:
- Hands-on sessions and mentor-led workshops  
- Real-world coding challenges  
- Networking with other Django and Python enthusiasts  

---

## 💡 The Hackathon Experience
After Django Day, the real adventure begins!  
**Build. Break. Innovate. Hackathon** kicks off on **October 22**, bringing together developers, designers, and innovators to work on exciting projects across multiple domains — from **AI and Web3** to **IoT and open-source tools**.

### 🕓 What’s in Store
- **Kickoff:** October 22 (Wednesday)  
- **Project Presentations:** The following morning, October 23  
- **Venue:** Rotary community Centre, Ho, Ghana  

Whether you’re working solo or in a team, we’re celebrating creativity and community-driven innovation.  
You don’t need to finish a perfect project — just come, build, and share what you create.

---

## 👩🏾‍💻 Who Can Participate?
Anyone with a PyHo 2025 ticket (in-person) is eligible!  
Bring your curiosity, your ideas, and your laptop — we’ll provide the rest.
