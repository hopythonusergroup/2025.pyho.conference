Welcome to Ho

We are happy to be hosting PyHo 2025 on October 24 & 25, 2025 at the G.R.N.M.A Hotel, Ho.

📍 Venue: G.R.N.M.A Hotel, Ho, Ghana

📅 Dates: Friday, October 24 – Saturday, October 25, 2025

🕘 Time: 9:00 AM – 4:40 PM each day
