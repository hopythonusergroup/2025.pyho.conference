About PyHo

Started in 2024, PyHo is a two days, single track conference by the Python User Group Ho.

PyHo is a regional Python conference in the Volta Region of Ghana, celebrates the developer community's activities, achievements, and contributions within and beyond. It is run by volunteers from the developer and Python community within and beyond the region.

Important Dates to Keep

  • Call for Speakers Opens: March 15, 2025
  • Registration/Ticket Sales Open: March 15, 2025
  • Call for Speakers Closes: June 15, 2025
  • Speakers Announced: July 5, 2025
  • Registration/Ticket Sales Close: September 20, 2025
  • Pre-Conference: Django Day & Hackathon October 22-23, 2025
  • Conference Days: October 24-25, 2025

Join us for the largest gathering of developers of all levels - enthusiasts, beginners, intermediates, and advanced professionals in Ho, Volta Region.

Code of Conduct

We're committed to providing a safe and friendly environment for everyone to learn, meet, and enjoy the conference. All organizers, attendees, speakers and sponsors are to follow the Code of Conduct.

Diversity & Inclusion

We encourage diversity, enforce our Code of Conduct, and make our event as inclusive and accessible as possible.
Our Opportunity Grants affirms our D & I goal.

Public Health Safety

As part of our commitment to the safety of our attendees, we have a Public Health & Safety Policy we request of all attendees to adhere to.
